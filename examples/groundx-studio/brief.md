# GroundX Studio Business Film Brief

## Product Position

GroundX reads complex enterprise documents and returns structured data, grounded answers, and source-backed reports. GroundX Studio is the in-development business-user surface for seeing and using those outputs without beginning from an API integration.

The film must not claim general customer availability.

## Canonical Experiences

- **Extract:** Pull fields, values, relationships, and context into consistent structured outputs.
- **Interact:** Ask questions and receive grounded answers with citations back to the source.
- **Report:** Assemble source-backed reports and continue exploring with follow-up questions.

Keep these names exactly. Do not invent replacement product labels.

## Audience

Write for business users, operations teams, and departmental users. Lead with usable information, reviewable evidence, and clearer decisions. Avoid implementation jargon unless the requested version targets engineers.

## Promise Boundaries

- GroundX Studio is in development.
- Do not promise immediate use, zero engineering lift, release timing, or general availability.
- Do not invent accuracy figures, implementation-speed claims, customer outcomes, or adoption claims.
- Do not introduce on-premises, air-gapped, residency, or deployment claims without consulting the current deployment owner documentation.

## Current Story Arc

1. Complex documents contain more usable knowledge than most systems expose.
2. GroundX reads the full page structure and preserves context.
3. Extract turns that structure into consistent data.
4. Interact provides grounded, source-linked answers.
5. Report turns evidence into material ready to share.
6. GroundX Studio brings those experiences into one focused business-user surface.
